02 / Featured project

University Student Healthcare App

A product concept focused on improving how students find and access healthcare support.

Product overview

Healthcare access, made easier for students.

A proposed mobile application designed to help university students access healthcare information and services more easily, from finding helpful resources to arranging appointments and receiving reminders.

01 / THE PROBLEM

Problem statement

Many university students experience difficulties accessing healthcare information, finding appropriate services and arranging consultations.

02 / THE AUDIENCE

Target audience

University students who need convenient access to healthcare information and services.

03 / THE SOLUTION

Proposed solution

A mobile application providing healthcare information, appointment booking, consultation access, health resources and medication reminders.

04 / PRODUCT GOALS

What success should look like

Improve access to healthcare services, make healthcare booking easier, improve student engagement and encourage continued use of the application.

03 / Measurement

Measuring product success

Metrics proposed for evaluating whether the concept helps students access healthcare services.

NORTH STAR METRIC

Weekly Active Students Who Successfully Access a Health Service

This metric focuses on meaningful use: students who are active and successfully access a healthcare service during the week.

Supporting KPIPurpose
Weekly Active UsersTrack usage of the application.
Booking Completion RateTrack successful appointment bookings.
Service Completion RateTrack completed healthcare services.
Retention RateTrack students who return to use the app.
Actions per Active StudentTrack engagement with app features.
04 / Growth

Building awareness and adoption

Proposed growth activities to reach students and encourage continued use.

Social awareness

Use TikTok and WhatsApp to introduce the app and share useful healthcare information.

Student ambassadors

Work with course representatives to share information and encourage student participation.

Healthcare partnerships

Collaborate with university healthcare centres to support service access and credibility.

Helpful reminders

Introduce relevant reminders and improve accessibility to support ongoing engagement.
